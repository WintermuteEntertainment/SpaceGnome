# SpaceGnome

A gnome, in space. Infinite falling platformer.

A 3D falling game where you play as a gnome tumbling through space towards Earth after his shuttle is wrecked. Land on drifting debris and asteroid rocks, drop your own dance floors, breakdance to build combos and boost, and recover your coins before time runs out. Built in Unity (URP) as a game development project by Wintermute Entertainment.
